About this list: This is a sampling of program announcements the school's college counseling office receives each year. Inclusion here is informational only and does not constitute an endorsement. It's the responsibility of students and families to research any program further before applying or paying. Corrections or additions can be sent to college.counseling@sbs.org.

A note on pre-college programs: Many of the programs marked "pre‑college" let students experience college life on a campus while taking low-pressure classes. If your main goal is admissions advantage, these are often a lower-value use of time and money than more selective, research- or merit-based programs — though they can still be worthwhile for skill-building (e.g. an arts portfolio).
